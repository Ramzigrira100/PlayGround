❓ How to play

🧑‍🌾 Peasants gather: select them → tap a 🪙 gold mine or 🌲 tree.
🏠 Town Hall trains peasants. Barracks trains ⚔️ Footmen & 🏹 Archers.
🦸 Train a Paladin hero (Town Hall): Heal (💚) + Fireball (🔥) abilities.
🗼 Guard Towers shoot enemies automatically. Farms raise army cap 🍖.
🏁 Selecting a building then tapping ground sets its rally point.
👹 Enemy waves come from the north-east. Kill scouts early for breathing room!
🖱️ PC: left-click = tap, drag = pan, wheel = zoom, WASD = camera, Esc = cancel.
